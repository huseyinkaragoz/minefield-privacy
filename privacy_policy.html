MineField Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

MineField is a Minesweeper game for Android and iOS, published by FunnIT. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. In short: it does not collect any.

Information we collect

None. MineField has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not connect to the internet. We do not collect, receive, store on our servers, sell or share any personal or usage data.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps a few values on your own device:

  • the concept and difficulty you played last,
  • your best time on each difficulty,
  • your sound, vibration and reduce-motion settings.

These stay on your device, are never sent anywhere, and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Your device's own backup (for example Google or iCloud backup) may include them, under that service's terms.

Children

MineField does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app does with information, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
